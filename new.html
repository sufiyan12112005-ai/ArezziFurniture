How Android behaves

Android notification behaviour depends on Chrome, Android version, permission settings, battery optimization and device settings. On Xiaomi/POCO devices, allow notifications and background activity where Android settings require it.

System notification sounds are controlled by Android/browser notification settings. This simulator cannot override silent mode, DND or notification-channel restrictions.

General

Future Push Backend

This project does not include a push server. Turning this on only stores configuration for future integration; it does not fake background push delivery.

Privacy & security

Schedules are stored locally in this browser unless you later connect a push service. Do not enter passwords, banking data, private credentials or real WhatsApp login information.
