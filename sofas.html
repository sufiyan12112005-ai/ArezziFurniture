Choosing a custom sofa for your room

Plan seating and space together

L-shape and sectional sofas can define a larger living area, while a loveseat or compact lounge sofa can suit a smaller room. Recliners need room behind and in front for movement. For a sofa cum bed, consider the floor space required when it is opened. Measure the wall, doorways and walking routes before deciding on the arrangement.

Explore fabrics, colours and construction

The feel and care needs of linen, velvet, leatherette and other upholstery options differ. Share how the sofa will be used, your preferred seat feel and any cleaning concerns.
