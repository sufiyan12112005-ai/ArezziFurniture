Our story

Furniture begins
with the way you live.

Arezzi Furniture is a furniture manufacturer and custom furniture specialist based in Bengaluru. With approximately 15 years of experience, our focus is on sofas, beds and tables made around individual requirements.

We work with customers on the choices that make a piece personal: its form, material, fabric, colour and dimensions. Whether you are planning a home or a commercial space, the conversation starts with what you need.
