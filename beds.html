Plan a bed around comfort, storage and access

Confirm the mattress and room measurements

Single, double, queen and king labels can cover different measurements. Start with your actual mattress size, then allow for the bed frame, headboard and space to walk beside it. Check doors, stairs and lift access when discussing a larger bed.

Choose practical storage and a finish you like

A hydraulic storage bed offers lift-up access, while box and drawer layouts have different access needs. Upholstered headboards bring a softer appearance, and visible wood frames offer another design direction. Discuss the mechanism, materials, finish and installation requirements with Arezzi before confirming your specifications.
